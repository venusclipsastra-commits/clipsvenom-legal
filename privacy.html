Privacy Policy

Effective September 12, 2026

Data used

When you connect TikTok, ClipsVenom uses the account identifier and authorization tokens needed to transfer videos you select. It processes local video files, generated titles, and upload status.

Purpose

This data is used only to authenticate the connected user, prepare clips, send selected videos to TikTok for manual review, and display operation status.

Storage

Video processing and application settings are kept on the operator's computer. Authorization credentials are intended to be stored locally and must not be exposed publicly. TikTok receives a video only when the transfer feature is used.

Sharing

ClipsVenom does not sell personal data. Information is shared with TikTok only as required to provide the requested connection and video-transfer functionality.

Retention and deletion

Local clips and credentials remain until the operator removes them or disconnects the account. TikTok access can be revoked through TikTok account settings.

Security

Reasonable local safeguards are used, but no system is completely secure. Client secrets and access tokens should never be posted publicly or included in screenshots.

Children

ClipsVenom is not intended for children and does not knowingly collect children's personal information.

Contact

Privacy questions may be submitted through the ClipsVenom support page.
